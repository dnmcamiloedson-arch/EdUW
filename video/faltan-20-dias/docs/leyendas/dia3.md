# Día 3 · Fíjate en el espejo (El Niño Fantasma del KM 31)

> A car camera records the fog at kilómetro 31 of the México–Toluca highway in one unbroken file. The opening tells you to watch the rear-view mirror, and the mirror keeps rewarding you: a seatbelt swings by itself, a small handprint appears, and a lullaby keeps humming after the radio is switched off. On the third wiper sweep a pale, dripping child is standing in the lane. The driver brakes, the file drops to black before anything touches, and the road is empty. Outside, small wet footprints lead to a forgotten roadside cross with a red tin toy car, and red eyes watch him from the fog while he prays. Back in the car the toy is on the back seat, and so is the child. Minutes later the driver comes back, returns the toy and lights the dead veladora: nobody had come back for him, until tonight. The child's small hand covers the 1 of the KM 31 post, and the 3 that is left becomes the countdown.

**Duración:** 48.5 s + outro

## Recurso

ONE RECOVERED DASHCAM FILE. EP03_LEN = 1320 frames (44.0 s), plus the standard 4.5 s outro (duration_s 48.5 includes it). The camera is bolted behind the mirror and never cuts. Every move comes from car physics: road buzz, seam bumps, curve roll, the brake nose-dive, the stall rebound, the door slam. The only breaks are file artifacts: the boot glitch and rewind, a 2-frame freeze and an 18-frame dropout at the brake, the black under the title, and one found-footage cut with a clock jump into the coda.
Four diegetic instruments inside one fixed frame tell the story.
(1) THE REAR-VIEW MIRROR is a second, mirror-reversed stage. The hook «Fíjate en el espejo.» trains the eye on it, and it pays out escalating clues: the seatbelt swings, a handprint appears, the hum comes from the back seat, a 6-frame silhouette, the red toy car, two red points. Then the main scare comes out of it.
(2) THE WIPER METRONOME. Intermittent sweeps at 6.0, 12.4 and 18.8 s make the third sweep expected, and that sweep wipes the child into existence.
(3) THE OSD (Alike, burned-in data). REC, the clock, km/h and the km reading reach 31.0 exactly at the curve exit. Speed falls to 0 under «FRENADO BRUSCO · ARCHIVO PROTEGIDO», and the clock jumps from 02:31:31 to 02:34:07 for the coda.
(4) THE HAZARD RELAY, a frame-locked 24-frame pulse (75 bpm, 1.25 Hz). It shows the child's body in the fog only on its pulses, and it sets the beat that the main scare breaks: the relay skips one tick and the face lunges 6 frames later, out of silence.
MISDIRECTION: you are told to watch the mirror. The first scare comes from the road, the dread from the fog, and the big scare from the mirror once you have stopped watching it.
PAYOFF: the post that names the legend gives the number. His small hand covers the 1, and the 3 that is left lifts off the concrete into the lockup.

## Sustos

- **t:** 0.13 · **what:** A 3-frame subliminal full-frame glitch of the pale face with red eyes, before the rewind. · **why_it_works:** It plants an image the viewer can't place. That open loop closes at 35.6 s with the same glitch language and the same face, which pays off rewatching.
- **t:** 8.6 · **what:** In the mirror, the rear seatbelt swings by itself. · **why_it_works:** It rewards «Fíjate en el espejo» within seconds. A small, physical wrongness with no explanation starts the dread.
- **t:** 14.9 · **what:** The radio is clicked off and its display reflection dies, but the lullaby goes on as a dry child's hum from the back seat and ends in a small wet inhale. · **why_it_works:** It breaks the logic of the sound source: the voice was never in the radio. Losing the AM filter and reverb brings it into the car, behind the driver, so the dread comes from closeness rather than volume. The dying display reflection carries the beat for muted viewers.
- **t:** 16.6 · **what:** A 6-frame, barely visible head-and-shoulders silhouette between the mirror headrests, in the silence of the curve. · **why_it_works:** It sits below conscious notice, so it is felt more than seen. Rewatchers find it, which drives replays and '¿lo viste?' comments.

He doesn't flinch, turns to face us, his eyes flash red as he fills the frame, and the file drops to black and digital silence before any contact. · **why_it_works:** Sweeps at 6.0 and 12.4 s make the third one expected, but not what it reveals. 3.6 s of silence removes the audio cue beforehand. His calm against our panic is the uncanny part. Cutting to nothing at peak sensory load lets the viewer feel the legend's 'pensaron que lo atropellaron' without ever seeing or hearing an impact.

## Revelación del número

The number comes from the legend's own name. The KM 31 post is planted at 18.0 s, sits in the right beam for the whole second half, and has the child's descanso at its foot. In the coda, after the driver has lit the veladora and left, a digital zoom pushes ×3.0 onto the post's numerals. The child's small wet hand curls around the edge from behind and covers the '1', and his half face peeks out beside it.
What's left is a 3, lit warm by his candle. At 41.2 s that 3 lifts off the concrete toward the camera. Its black paint turns to gold with an ember glow, and over 12 frames its shape morphs from the post's Alike glyph to the Rye glyph of the lockup. It lands at about 600 px tall, centered at (540, 920).
LeyendaLockup builds around it: «FALTAN» above, «DÍAS» and «28 · 10» below, all between y 470 and about 1470. Behind it, his eye closes and the flame stays.
The meaning: he is still at 31, and in 3 days the altars (his own is altar 4, Medicina, credited under the title) will light the candle he has waited for.

## Música

An original score in D minor resolving to D major, almost entirely diegetic. Melody: the traditional lullaby 'Señora Santa Ana' (anonymous, public domain; a song about a crying child), in a fresh arrangement, never sung with words. It is unused elsewhere in the series: 'A la roro niño' is Día 6, 'La Llorona' Día 2, 'Las Golondrinas' Días 4 and 0, 'Cielito Lindo' Días 5 and 0.

(5) METRONOME: the hazard relay at exactly 24 frames (75 bpm), 21.8–34.6 s. Its missing tick at 35.4 s is the score's held breath.
