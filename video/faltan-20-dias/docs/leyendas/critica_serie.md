## Arco hacia los días 1 y 0

The arc is strong and should be protected: monsters first, then the realisation that the legends are about our dead, then the true visit.

1) The spine.
   - Día 8: the abuela's voice is a chaneque's lie («Mi abuela murió en marzo. Y aun así, contesté.»).
   - Día 2 turns terror into tenderness («…sin saber que nunca se fueron.», with a Picardy third).
   - Día 1 makes the viewer the missing one («Tú.» → «Mañana te esperan.»).
   - Día 0 answers Día 8: the real abuela hums back «…canta y no llores.»
   - Make the bookend explicit by changing Día 0's first card to «Abuela, tú decías:». Optional, almost free: give the nieta a terracotta listón knotted on her left wrist, so viewers who followed from Día 8 feel the echo without anyone claiming she is the same family.
2) A thread worth keeping visible: remembering someone lights a flame.
   - 4: a veladora lights on the forgotten niche.
   - 3: «Nadie volvió por él. Hasta esta noche.» and the relit candle.
   - 2: the children's two velitas.
   - 1: the cirio, then the seven altar lights, with altar 4 last.
   - 0: every window in the city, the seven altars on the Santa Fe ridge, and then the logo.
   With Día 4's fix (the veladora warms the carved 4 in place), the series hands this image forward for five days in a row.
3) Día 1 must be the visual sum of its episodes. After the fixes:
   - Brujas (3 orbs, emptied rebozo, mirror and crossed scissors) = Día 6.
   - Nahual (old man with sombrero and cane whose brim splits into ears, then coyote) = Día 7.
   - Charro (void under the brim with ember eyes, coin with a sun reverse, «No recojas nada.» echoing «No recojas su oro.») = Día 5.
   - Niño (headlights with no car, red eyes, red tin car, the eye closing that lights altar 4) = Día 3. He is kept dry on purpose.
   - Momias (knocks from a sealed gaveta, rosary on the wrist, no vitrina, no bandage strips) = Día 4.
   - Llorona (veil, hands, distant lament, the son on a music box) = Día 2.
   - Chaneques (palm-leaf hat, broad ears, gold eyes, muddy footprints, imitated whisper) = Día 8.
   Each sits at its own altar with the students' traces (cinta canela, the ladder, «Los altares quedaron listos.»): Ala Sur has 1–3 and Ala Norte has 4–7, and the date slug reads 27·10. Build the seven rigs as shared modules with their own episodes (the Día 3 notes already call for nino.ts, the shared rig for the Niño).
4) Intensity curve. 8→2 escalate in scale and closeness. Día 2 lands soft so Día 1 can be a reunion with one big jolt, and Día 0's slam sits 4 dB under the legend scares and turns into love on the dog's first wag. Keep that order. Don't add a scare to Día 0, and don't soften Día 1's «Tú.».
5) Music and key arc. A D drone ties 8, 7, 6, 4, 3, 1 and 0 together; 2 (E minor) and 5 (A minor) are the cold outliers.
