# Día 2 · Búscala en el agua

> At night in a colonial alley we carry a single candle, followed by a lament that always sounds far away. The street lamps die one by one. Far ahead, a woman in white appears under the last one, and when we get close she vanishes. In the water she keeps getting closer: in every puddle, and finally right behind our own reflection in the river. Her scream comes out of the water and the candle goes out. When it relights by itself, the river shows what she has never been able to see: the two small lights of her children have been following her the whole time.

**Duración:** 48.5 s + outro

## Recurso

EL ESPEJO DEL AGUA. The viewer is the walker: a POV camera at 1.6 m eye height. The only visible part of us is our right hand holding a short candle in a barro holder, which is our only warm light once the faroles die. Two rules contradict each other, and that contradiction is the dread. (1) EYE: up close, she exists only in water. Looked at directly she vanishes (the chase ends in a one-frame vanish). In the puddle and in the river every glance down shows her closer, and every glance up shows an empty callejón. The cut between up and down keeps the puddle at exactly the same screen position, so the eye compares the two views instantly. (2) EAR: the hook states the real folk saying 'si la oyes lejos, es porque ya está cerca'. For 30 s the lament is always mixed far away (low-passed, wet, about −24 dB) even while the reflections bring her to our feet. The lament text says the same thing silently: it shrinks and fades each time (46 px, then 36 px). At the river the lament stops completely. When it returns, it is dry, full-band and in our face, and the same line slams in huge Rye: the jump scare. (3) MUSIC: a lone requinto begins the son 'La Llorona' and is cut mid-phrase on its suspended note, on the frame the first farol dies. The melody stays unresolved until the candle relights. It then resumes from that exact note and resolves to major over the payoff. The loop closes with water: the first image and sound are a drop into a puddle, and the last are her tear falling into the river, inside the numeral '2' formed by floating cempasúchil petals and her children's two lights.

## Dirección de arte

'Nocturno de agua y vela'. NIGHT GRADE anchored in the brand palette. Moonlight is a cold teal-blue key from the upper left (mids around #163640). COLOUR GRAMMAR: warm means love, the children and home: the faroles (gold #F5C242) until they die, then only our candle (core #F5C242, falloff #F26D3D, a 750 px pool that flickers ±6% at 7 Hz + 1.3 Hz), the petal path (#F26D3D / #F5C242), the two warm windows on the far bank, the children's lights and finally the numeral. She is the only near-white object in the film and is never pure white. The bloom threshold is set so that only she, the candle and the faroles bloom.
